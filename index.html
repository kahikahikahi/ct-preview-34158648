What is it and what’s in it?

Everything here is made in-house.

Falafel: Chickpeas, onion, capsicum, celery, coriander and spices GF, DF, V

Mujver: Zucchini, pumpkin, carrot, onion, corn, eggs, herbs, spices and flour DF

Chicken Doner: We use Tegel chicken breast and boneless chicken thighs finely sliced and marinated overnight in homemade marinade with a yoghurt base, skewered on doner skewer and cooked doner style GF

Lamb Doner: We use premium halal lamb mince finely ground and marinated overnight in homemade marinade GF, DF

Chicken Skewer: We use Tegel chicken boneless chicken thighs cut into cubes and marinated overnight in homemade marinade then skewered (120g each skewer) GF

Lamb Skewer: We use AFFCO or Southern Halal Lamb Loin (lamb backstrap) cut into cubes and marinated overnight in homemade marinade with a yoghurt base, then skewered (90g each skewer) GF

Turkish Bread: Yoghurt in dough and egg used for glazing, sprinkled with sesame seeds

Baklava: Walnut filled filo pastry, cooked in oven and drizzled with homemade syrup. Contains butter.

Honey Pumpkin Dessert: Peeled pumpkin cooked in a herb syrup and topped with tahini and served with ice cream or cream

Hummus: Made from chickpeas, tahini, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic and spices GF, DF, V

Beetroot: Yoghurt drained overnight and mixed with beetroot, garlic, olive oil and spices GF

Cacik: Yoghurt drained overnight and mixed with cucumber, mint, garlic, olive oil, and spices GF

Tabbouleh (Kisir): Made from bulgur wheat cooked in olive oil with tomato paste, vegetables and spices and finished with fresh parsley, tomato, cucumber DF, V

Spicy Walnut (Muhammara): Made from roasted red peppers, sun dried tomato, tahini, garlic, herbs, spices and olive oil.

Sigara Borek: Filo pastry filled with feta and parsley then fried until golden.
