Privacy

Café Turquoise is at 81 Devon Street West, New Plymouth.

When you place an order or book a table, we collect your name, phone number, email, and the details of your order or booking.

We use this to prepare your order, manage your booking, and contact you about them.

This information is stored securely. Only our staff can see it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone else except Stripe, who processes payments.

Your phone or browser can remember your name, phone number and email for next time. You can clear these by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information by contacting us on 06 757 8921.

This page is our notice under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.
